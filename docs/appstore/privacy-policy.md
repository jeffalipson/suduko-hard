# Really Really Hard Sudoku — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

Really Really Hard Sudoku does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information.

- **No accounts.** The app doesn't ask who you are.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking code.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Games in progress, settings, and statistics are saved only on your iPhone. They are removed if you delete the app.
- **No network access.** The app works entirely offline.
- **Sharing.** If you use Share after a solve, the text is passed to the share sheet you choose; the app itself sends nothing.

Questions: contact the developer at the support address listed on the App Store page.
